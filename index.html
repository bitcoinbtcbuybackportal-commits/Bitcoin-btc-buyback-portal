06 / FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What is the BTC buyback program? +

The portal provides information about the BTC program, allocation calculation and participation through the deployed BNB Smart Chain contract.

How is my BTC allocation calculated? +

The portal can read the deployed contract's calculateBTC function and use its returned base, bonus and total values.

What network is being used? +

The contract is deployed for use on the BNB Smart Chain. Always verify the network and contract address before approving a transaction.

Where can I verify the contract? +

The complete contract address is displayed in the participation section with a direct BscScan link.

What happens when I click Buy BTC? +

The website prepares a call to the deployed contract's payable buyBTC function. Your compatible Web3 wallet must approve the transaction before it is sent to the blockchain.
