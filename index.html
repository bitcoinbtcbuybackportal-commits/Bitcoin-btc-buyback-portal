Understand the process.

A clear overview of the portal and the steps involved before interacting with your connected wallet.

01

Choose your BNB amount

The participation calculator accepts amounts from 5 BNB through 1000 BNB. Enter your intended amount to calculate the corresponding BTC allocation.

02

Review your allocation

The calculator displays the base BTC amount, the configured 11% bonus, and the resulting total BTC allocation.

03

Connect your preferred wallet

Connect a compatible Web3 wallet on BNB Smart Chain. Your wallet is used to review and authorize transactions rather than entering private wallet information into the portal.

04

Verify the destination

Copy the contract address displayed below and compare it with the destination shown inside your wallet before approving any transaction.

05

BNB Smart Chain

The configured network is BNB Smart Chain with Chain ID 56. Make sure your connected wallet is using the correct network before proceeding.

06

Confirm from your wallet

Before confirming, review the transaction amount, destination contract, network, and any applicable wallet fees directly in your wallet.
