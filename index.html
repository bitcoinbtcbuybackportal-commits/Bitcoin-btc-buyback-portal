FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What network is used?

The configured contract is on BNB Smart Chain, chain ID 56.

How is the BTC amount calculated?

The calculator calls the contract's calculateBTC function and displays its returned base, bonus and total amounts.

What is the allocation bonus?

The portal reads the configured bonus percentage from the contract. The current configured fallback value is 11%.

Where does activity come from?

Activity is loaded from confirmed BTCPurchased contract events.
